    Before you convert

    Complex SVG filters, CSS and external fonts may look different. External images and scripts are not supported. Check important documents after conversion. PDF text may become paths; links, forms and animation are not preserved.

    Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Limits: 20 files, 50 MB each, 100 MB total, and 100 pages per PDF. Complex files may take longer.

    WEBP is an image format: selectable text and vector editing are not preserved. WEBP to PDF creates one PDF per image. Transparency becomes white; animation uses the first frame.

    PDF to TXT combines selected pages into one UTF-8 file. Pages without text use OCR in the selected language, which takes longer and may misread small text or handwriting. TXT to PDF wraps UTF-8 text on A4 (200,000 characters, 100 pages). TIFF output is uncompressed and can be large; multipage TIFF becomes one multipage PDF.
